# Upstream attribution and adaptation

Formative's interview and shared-language guidance adapts Matt Pocock's `grilling` and `domain-modeling` skills, the methods composed by `grill-with-docs`.

Source repository: https://github.com/mattpocock/skills
Pinned source commit: `a496f5513d4a4cf889bc48e189e579f4b0bdf6aa` (retrieved 2026-10-30).

- [Grilling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/a496f5513d4a4cf889bc48e189e579f4b0bdf6aa/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md)
- [Domain modeling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/a496f5513d4a4cf889bc48e189e579f4b0bdf6aa/skills/engineering/domain-modeling/SKILL.md)
- [Original MIT license](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/a496f5513d4a4cf889bc48e189e579f4b0bdf6aa/LICENSE); included locally as [LICENSE-mattpocock.txt](LICENSE-mattpocock.txt).

The adapted guidance ships inside Formative. No external skill lookup or installation is required at runtime. Review upstream changes deliberately; do not automatically replace the interview during a user's session.

Retained: persistent decision-tree exploration, dependency-aware questioning, recommendations, evidence gathering, shared-understanding confirmation, precise domain terms, concrete scenarios, and recording consequential decisions as they emerge.

Adaptations: use manageable question rounds rather than requiring every currently answerable question at once; track blocked and explicitly deferred branches; allow scoped frontend experiments before agreement; resume discovery after prototypes; preserve user stop/delegation choices; use available research tools without requiring a particular host's Skill tool or research subagents. Documentation follows the project's needs instead of mandating glossary/ADR files for every idea. Final plans are agent-authored and independent of prototype styling. Prototype feedback still uses its own dedicated worker.
